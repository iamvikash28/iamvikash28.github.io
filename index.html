About

Built on the sales floor,
refined with data.

Before I wrote a line of SQL, I was closing out a jewellery store's books by hand — reconciling the day's sales, flagging slow-moving stock, and trying to remember which customers kept coming back. That's still my day job. What's changed is the toolkit: the same questions I used to answer with a calculator and a notebook, I now answer with SQL, Power BI, and Python.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science from JSS Academy of Technical Education, with a Diploma and coursework toward an M.Tech in Data Science. Over the past year I've used that foundation to build three end-to-end BI projects — spanning retail, jewellery, and customer analytics — designed to show the range a BI team actually needs: querying, modeling, and reporting that holds up when someone asks "why?"
